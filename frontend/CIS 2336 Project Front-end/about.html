About us

A home base for students on the Sugar Land campus, run by students and staff who want your day here to be a little easier.

Why we're here

Many UH Sugar Land students commute, work, and take classes all in the same day. Between classes, you need somewhere to go: a quiet place to study, a room for your group, or just a comfortable seat and a coffee.

The Student Center brings all of that into one building. We run five bookable rooms, an open lounge, a café, and events all semester long.

Our goal is simple: every student should feel welcome here, whether you're in your first semester or your last.
