Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what students ask us most. Select a question to see the answer.

Who can use the Student Center?

Any current University of Houston student can use the lounge, café, and rooms. To book a room, you'll need your UH email and your 7-digit UH ID.

How do I book a study or meeting room?

Go to Book a room, choose a room, date, and time, and we'll tell you right away if it's free. Fill in your details and you'll get a confirmation code. Show it at the front desk when you arrive.

How long can I keep a room, and how far ahead can I book?

You can book a room for 30 minutes up to 3 hours at a time, starting on the hour or half hour. You can book up to 30 days ahead.

What if I'm running late or need to cancel?

We hold your room for 15 minutes. After that, it goes back to the pool so other students can use it. To cancel, send us a message with your confirmation code, or tell the front desk.

Is there food, Wi-Fi, and printing?

Yes to all three. Free Wi-Fi works throughout the building, the café serves coffee and food on weekdays, and there's a printer by the front desk that uses your Cougar Card.

Still have a question? Contact us and we'll get back to you within one school day.
