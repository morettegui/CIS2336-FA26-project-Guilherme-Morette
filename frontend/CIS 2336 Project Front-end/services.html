Lounge and café

No booking needed. The lounge has comfy chairs, big tables, phone chargers, and free Wi-Fi. It's the easiest place on campus to catch up with friends between classes.

The café serves coffee, tea, breakfast tacos, sandwiches, and snacks at student prices.

Café hours

  • Monday to Thursday: 7:30 AM – 7:00 PM
  • Friday: 7:30 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Saturday and Sunday: closed

Bring your own cup and get 25 cents off any drink.
